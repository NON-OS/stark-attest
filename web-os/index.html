WHAT A VERDICT PROVES

ok means: this trailer is a valid STARK membership proof under the root above, bound to exactly the measurement, capability mask and epoch shown on its row, at the parameters compiled into the verifier. the wasm is the deployment's own verify_attestation_trailer, built from NON-OS/stark-attest, reproducible bit for bit.

it does not prove the binary you run is this one. that link is the blake3 column, checked with b3sum against a release. a capability listed here is one the proof itself binds, not a line in a config file.

PARAMETERS

width-8 poseidon, 32 rounds, x^7 s-box. round constants are blake3 of a public string, re-derived in CI on every push; the mds matrix is cauchy, mds by theorem. fri at 32 queries, 16 grinding bits, blowup 4. soundness published under the provable floor and the conjectured model, side by side. no elliptic curves; post-quantum by construction.
